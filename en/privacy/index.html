Privacy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Knips is a disposable camera for iPhone. This page explains what the app does with your photos and any other information.

The party responsible for your data is Knips, run by Stian Gaare Vaagan. Questions can be sent to kontakt@knipspics.no.

The short version

  • Knips doesn’t collect any information about you. There’s no account, no server, and no sign-in.
  • Your photos stay on your phone. Knips doesn’t send them anywhere.
  • No ads, no tracking, and no analytics tools.

Your photos

Photos are stored in the app’s own folder on your phone. They don’t show up in Photos until you tap “Save to Photos” after a roll has been developed. Before a photo is saved, the app removes all the information the camera writes into the file, including the place where the photo was taken.

That folder is included in your phone’s regular backup (iCloud or your computer), which you control in your phone’s settings. If you delete the app, any photos you haven’t saved to Photos are deleted with it.

Purchases

Express developing is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Knips only finds out that a purchase went through, not who you are or how you paid. See Apple’s privacy policy for what Apple stores.

Permissions on your phone

  • Camera – to take photos. Only the back camera is used.
  • Photos – only to add the photos you choose to save after developing. Knips can’t see the photos you already have in Photos.
  • Notifications – a notification from your phone itself when a roll has been developed.

Your rights

Knips has no information about you outside your own phone, so there’s nothing for you to request access to or have deleted from us. If you have questions, write to kontakt@knipspics.no. If you believe your information is being handled in violation of the rules, you can file a complaint with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
