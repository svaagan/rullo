Knips

A disposable camera in your pocket

Knips turns your iPhone into a retro disposable camera. You snap away without seeing your photos, drop off the roll, and wait 24 hours for it to be developed – just like the old days. Then you get your photos with that real disposable-camera look: warm colors, grain, flash, and the occasional light leak.

How it works

  1. Take out the camera. A roll holds 27 photos, and you have to wind the film before every shot.
  2. When the roll is full – or whenever you decide to drop it off – it goes off to be developed.
  3. 24 hours later you get a notification, and the whole roll opens at once.
  4. Save the photos you want to Photos, or share them.

Free film

Rolls and developing are free, and a new roll is always ready right away. No account, no subscription, and no ads. If you can’t wait, you can buy express developing for one roll: ready in one hour for 19 NOK, or right now for 29 NOK. The App Store shows the price in your local currency.
