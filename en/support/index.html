Help

If you can’t find your answer here, write to kontakt@knipspics.no.

Why can’t I see my photos?

Just like with a real disposable camera, you can’t see your photos until the roll has been developed. Drop off the roll in the camera – or fill it up – and it’s ready 24 hours later. “My rolls” shows the countdown.

The shutter button is gray

You have to wind the film before every photo: tap or swipe the wind wheel. If you’re holding your phone upright, turn it sideways – Knips only takes landscape photos.

Can I take a selfie?

Knips only uses the back camera. Turn your phone sideways and press one of the volume buttons.

How much does it cost?

Film and developing are free, and you can shoot as many rolls as you like. If you don’t want to wait 24 hours, you can buy express developing for one roll: ready in one hour, or right now. The price is shown in your own currency before you buy.

I bought express, but my roll hasn’t been developed

Purchases that are waiting for approval (for example “Ask to Buy” in Family Sharing) are applied as soon as Apple approves them. If the roll was already developed by then, the purchase is used on the next roll you drop off.

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your phone, and in your phone’s regular backup. Save the photos you want to keep to Photos: if you delete Knips, any photos that exist only in the app are deleted with it.
